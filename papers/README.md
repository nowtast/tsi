# Public Manuscripts

Only the following bilingual manuscript pairs are canonical in this release.

| ID | English | Korean | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 01 | `paper1/` | `paper1_ko/` | R4-reviewed formal theory technical report |
| Paper 03 | `paper3/` | `paper3_ko/` | Reviewed sealed empirical record |
| Paper 04 | `paper4/` | `paper4_ko/` | Reviewed prospective attribution study |

Paper 02 and its topology, geometry, category, dynamics, and integration
components were developmental manuscripts. Their relevant formal content was
used during the research process, but those files are not standalone public
papers and are not tracked in this release. Paper 05 journal-submission packages
are venue-specific snapshots rather than canonical sources and likewise remain
outside the public tree.

Generated PDFs are included for convenient reading. The TeX sources and
section files are authoritative. Structural parity between each English/Korean
pair is checked by `tools/check_bilingual_parity.py`.

The canonical Paper 01 source is the formal manuscript closed after review
round 4 on 11 August 2026 and bibliographically corrected on 20 August 2026.
The correction adds a dedicated positioning section and 20 fully cited
references. The earlier position-paper draft is retained only in the ignored
local submission archive and is not part of the public release.
