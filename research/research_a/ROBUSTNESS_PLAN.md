# Research A2 Robustness Boundary

This plan was recorded before generation of the A1 confirmatory seed. It fixes
the directions and interpretation of A2, but it is not yet a numeric frozen A2
contract. Implementation, collision proofs, development-only power, and named
multiplicity members must precede a separate A2 seed.

## Candidate width

The generic selector will search 55, 100, and 300 output-feature positions at
prespecified low-sample prefixes. Every dictionary contains the true seven-term
support. Additional features must be deterministic functions of the same raw
state and action visible to both arms. This axis tests search-width sensitivity,
not representational superiority.

## Training noise

Training noise levels are 0.08, 0.30, 0.60, and 0.80. They remain below the
mathematical boundary 6/7 at which the true center loses its unique modal
status. Held-out noise remains 0.12. This axis tests degradation as the q-ary
mode gap closes.

## Misspecification in both directions

The scope audit includes a matched condition, a new fourth-family generator
outside the typed class but present in the generic dictionary, and a condition
where the generating family remains in typed search but is removed from the
generic dictionary. These are representability-boundary tests. They cannot
rescue a failed efficiency claim.

## Program stopping rule

If neither A1 nor the separately frozen A2 width/noise axes show a joint
SESOI-qualified advantage, predictive-efficiency development stops and the
turnover and perception studies do not begin. Research D remains available
because its model-audit estimand is independent of predictor efficiency.
